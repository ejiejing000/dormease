About DORMEASE

DORMEASE is a public room-discovery site with a private boarding-house management system behind it. Finding a room, becoming a tenant and managing a house are three separate processes.

Three access areas

Public website

No login: browse rooms, filter by price and type, view house information and submit a reservation request.

Tenant portal

Accounts provisioned by staff: monthly rent and utility bills, itemised receipts, payment history, maintenance tickets and announcements.

Landlord & staff

Authorised accounts only: tenants, rooms, reservation approval, bill generation, utility rates, tickets, staff assignment and audit history.

Smart Utility Billing

Billing starts when someone becomes an official tenant — not when a visitor submits a request. Staff record water and electricity meter readings; the system applies tiered rates, house fixed charges, move-in proration and roommate shares, then issues an itemised bill with a due date. Recorded payments update the balance and produce a receipt.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse or request a room. You need the account staff provision for you to see bills, download receipts and track maintenance tickets. There is no public registration for landlords or staff, and the login page never asks you to choose a role — the server decides from the account record.
