How DORMEASE works

1. Browse

Anyone can view rooms, prices and availability. No account, no role selection.

2. Request

Send your name, contact number and preferred move-in date for the room you like.

3. Approval

Only an authorised landlord or staff member approves or rejects requests.

4. Tenant account

Approved applicants receive a one-time link to set a password, then see bills and receipts.

You cannot register as a landlord or staff member. Management accounts are created by an existing landlord, and tenant accounts are provisioned by landlord or staff after approval.
