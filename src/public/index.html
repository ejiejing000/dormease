How DormEase works

Reserve a room

Pick a room, choose your move-in date and send a request. The landlord confirms and assigns the room online.

Landlord logs sub-meters

Each month your caretaker enters the water and electricity sub-meter readings for your specific room.

Itemized digital receipt arrives

DormEase calculates tiered utility rates, binds your rent, and sends you a transparent itemized receipt.

A bill you can actually read

Every charge is on its own line: your room space rent, the water you used, the electricity you used, and the tiered rate. No disputes, and you can print or save it as a digital receipt.
